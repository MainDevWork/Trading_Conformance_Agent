# Order types and order states

- **Market** (`order_type` 1): executes at the best available prices. In continuous trading any remainder expires; in an auction it waits for the uncross and any remainder then expires.
- **Limit** (2): executes at its limit price or better; the remainder rests in the book or expires according to its time in force.
- **Stop** (3) and **Stop Limit** (4): wait unelected (not in the book) until the stop price is reached, then become a Market or Limit order. Changing the stop price loses price and time priority.
- **Cross order** (Native message `C`, `order_type` 2, `time_in_force` 0 only, continuous trading only): both sides of a pre-agreed trade entered by one firm; executes immediately against itself. `cross_type` 5 fixed price (rejected if outside the allowed range), 50 price adjustable (moved to the mid-point of the best bid and offer).
- **Pegged hidden** (50) and **pegged hidden limit** (51): not visible; pegged to the mid-point (`order_sub_type` 50), best bid (51, buy only) or best offer (52, sell only); `display_quantity` 0; `minimum_quantity` (minimum execution size, MES) is required and must be at least the instrument's minimum reserve size (MRS, the number of shares worth R10 million at the previous closing price); the hard limit of a pegged limit order is in `stop_price`. Pegged orders can be entered only in continuous trading; in auction sessions they are parked and do not take part. A pegged order whose remainder falls below MRS after a partial fill is removed.
- **Iceberg order**: any order type with `display_quantity` above 0 and below `order_quantity`. Only the visible part is shown; when it is used up, the JSE refills it with a random amount and gives it a new `public_order_id`. Requires Protocol Version 2 or higher. In auctions the whole quantity takes part. (Volume 01E section 3.14 says the Basic gateway rejects a `display_quantity` other than 0 or the order quantity; Volume 201 test T20 and Volume 00E section 8.1.8 describe iceberg orders on the Native gateway, so treat such orders as icebergs.)
- **Visible order**: `display_quantity` equal to `order_quantity`.
- **Open order**: has quantity remaining in the book; can be amended or cancelled. **Parked order**: accepted but not yet active (GFA, GFX, ATC, CPX orders before their session; unelected stop orders). **Passive order**: resting in the book. **Aggressive order**: trades against resting orders on arrival.
- **Amendments**: amendable fields are order quantity, minimum execution size, limit price, stop price, expiry time (GTT), expiry date (GTD), client account. An order loses time priority if its quantity is increased, its limit price changes, or (iceberg) its display quantity is increased. Fully filled orders cannot be amended. Amending a partly filled visible order so that nothing remains is rejected (code 3000 "Invalid order size (< filled size)").
- **Matching priority** in continuous trading: price, then visibility (visible before hidden), then time.
